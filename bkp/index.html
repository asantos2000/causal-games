About Causal Games

Founded in 2026, Causal Games is focused on cause-and-effect fun: tight mechanics, instant feedback, and replayable loops. We target short session games that run smoothly on desktop and mobile, with zero paywalls and no in-app purchases.

We are designing our production pipeline around the same quality and performance bar expected by Poki: quick load times, clear first interaction, accessible controls, and high retention from the first minute.

How Our Studio Matches What Poki Looks For

1) Fast Start, Low Friction

Our games are designed to reach first meaningful interaction in seconds, with minimal text, fast loading, and tutorial-by-doing.

2) Strong Core Loop

We structure each game around a clear repeatable loop with escalating challenge and satisfying retries to maximize session quality.

3) Web Performance Discipline

Mobile/tablet support, responsive 16:9 play area, stable frame pacing, and iterative optimization are built into development from day one.

4) Family-Friendly by Default

We intentionally avoid unsafe themes and produce inclusive, wholesome experiences suitable for broad audiences.

5) Data-Informed Iteration

We plan game iteration around funnel metrics and playtest feedback, aligning with Player Fit and Web Fit expectations.

6) Thumbnail-to-Gameplay Consistency

Our visual identity and first gameplay moments are designed to align tightly, improving expectation match from click to play.

Current game in development

Kluster (working concept)

A magnetic dexterity game where each move can trigger chain reactions. Players place magnetic stones inside a dynamic boundary and must avoid accidental clusters. The physics-first design creates tension, surprise, and highly replayable "one more round" sessions.

  • Physics-driven magnetic interactions with readable feedback
  • Short rounds with clear fail states and immediate retry loop
  • Accessible controls for desktop and mobile play
  • Scope tailored for rapid browser iteration and performance tuning
